About Me

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Theory of Computation (ToC) Lab, Yonsei University. My research bridges formal language theory and natural language processing (NLP), with a focus on topics such as M-unambiguous languages, language sparsity, and probability convergence. On the NLP side, I work on few-shot learning, self-training techniques, and grammar-based data augmentation. I’m nearing the end of my PhD and have published multiple papers in both theoretical and applied domains.

Research

  • M-equivalence, strong M-equivalence, and language sparsity
  • Formal language theory and computational complexity
  • Natural language processing (NLP) with a focus on few-shot learning and self-training techniques
  • Grammar-based data augmentation and its application in text classification
  • Parikh matrix mapping and its relevance to computational linguistics
